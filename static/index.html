Turn messy WhatsApp orders into a clean order sheet.

Parchi reads order messages in Hinglish or English, works out the bill, and lists what to make tomorrow. It runs on your own laptop, so your customers' names and numbers never leave it.

The model only reads the messages. Prices, totals and the prep list are worked out by plain code. Anything unclear is marked Check for you to look at.

On a laptop without a GPU each message takes about 15 to 20 seconds. Results are saved, so reading the same message again is instant.
